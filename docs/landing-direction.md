# StrivePay landing direction

## Product and hierarchy

The main action is creating a personal or business account. The page explains independent bank-to-crypto and crypto-to-bank journeys, then lets visitors explore funding coverage before registration. Verification precedes financial activity. Receiving wallets belong to the user's selected setup; the landing page does not imply compulsory custody.

Five content groups: proposition and original 3D sculpture; interactive buy/sell preview; coverage explorer; three setup steps; FAQ and final CTA. No fabricated volume, customer proof, rates, settlement guarantees or regulatory claims.

## Style kernel

Geist sans; restrained 500-weight display type; 94px desktop hero, 60–86px mobile; 43–60px section headings. Warm white #f8f9f5, ink #142b32, teal #087e83, pale green #e8ede6. 6–12px corner family. 1232px content, 1376px feature panels. 8/16/24/32/48/64/96 spacing. Tabler outline icons. Physical silver/teal Blender sculpture with studio lighting. Motion is used for mode changes and globe rotation, with a reduced-motion fallback.

## Interaction and QA plan

Buy/sell buttons update illustrative product instructions, without submitting transfers. Coverage country search and buttons must work with a keyboard and on mobile; unsupported coverage is explained. FAQ uses native details. CTAs resolve to existing /register and /login pages. Visible focus and semantic headings are required.

Review actual dashboard in provided test account, then inspect the implemented landing at 375, 430, 768, 1440 and 1920px. Capture rendered mockups for review. Run typecheck and production build, plus focused interaction checks; confirm no horizontal overflow, broken images, inaccessible coverage controls, or provider names in public copy. Keep credentials out of saved artifacts and screenshots intended for the public landing.
